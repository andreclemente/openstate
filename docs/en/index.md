      <h2 class="os-section-label">The problem</h2>
      <p class="os-section-intro">Someone changes address. Updates it with the tax authority, social security, healthcare. The same information, three times, three systems that don't communicate.</p>
      <p class="os-section-intro os-section-intro-strong">This is not an isolated case. It is a pattern. But there's no structure to document it.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="os-two-col-col">
      <h2 class="os-section-label">The response</h2>
      <p class="os-section-intro">Every situation documented with evidence becomes part of a permanent memory — verifiable, open, accessible.</p>
      <p class="os-section-intro os-section-intro-strong">We don't promise to fix anything. We promise the knowledge won't be lost.</p>
