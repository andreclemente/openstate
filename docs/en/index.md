<!-- SECTION 1: WHY IT EXISTS -->
<div class="os-section" id="why">
  <div class="os-section-inner">
    <h2 class="os-section-label">Why it exists</h2>
    <p class="os-section-intro">Every day, millions of people interact with public services. Many face unnecessary complexity, repeated processes, or avoidable obstacles. These situations are discussed, forgotten, and repeated — because there is no structure connecting them.</p>
    <div class="os-cards">
      <div class="os-card">
        <h3>Isolated experiences</h3>
        <p>When someone waits 8 months for an appointment, it is a personal problem. When thousands wait the same but nobody connects the cases, it becomes an invisible pattern.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card">
        <h3>Fragmented information</h3>
        <p>Problems in healthcare, education, and transport are rarely documented together. Each sector lives in its own silo — and cross-sector patterns go undiscovered.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card">
        <h3>No collective memory</h3>
        <p>Without structured documentation, every problem seems new. Little is learned from what already happened. The same frictions repeat year after year.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 2: THE RESPONSE -->
<div class="os-section" id="what">
  <div class="os-section-inner">
    <h2 class="os-section-label">What OpenState does</h2>
    <p class="os-section-intro">OpenState turns scattered experiences into structured knowledge. It does not propose solutions. It does not take sides. It documents what happens, so patterns become visible.</p>
    <div class="os-cards">
      <div class="os-card os-card-accent">
        <div class="os-card-num">01</div>
        <h3>Observe</h3>
        <p>Records what happens in a public service — factually, reproducibly, with verifiable evidence.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card os-card-accent">
        <div class="os-card-num">02</div>
        <h3>Document</h3>
        <p>Structures each observation with context, impact, and source — so anyone can verify and understand.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card os-card-accent">
        <div class="os-card-num">03</div>
        <h3>Connect</h3>
        <p>Links related observations — so isolated problems reveal systemic patterns over time.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 3: WHAT IT IS NOT -->
<div class="os-section os-section-not" id="not">
  <div class="os-section-inner">
    <h2 class="os-section-label">What it is not</h2>
    <div class="os-cards os-cards-not">
      <div class="os-card os-card-dim">
        <span class="os-card-x">✕</span>
        <h3>A complaint platform</h3>
        <p>No venting. No anonymous reports. Only factual observations with evidence.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card os-card-dim">
        <span class="os-card-x">✕</span>
        <h3>A political initiative</h3>
        <p>No ideology. No parties. OpenState is neutral by design — it does not propose reforms or take positions.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card os-card-dim">
        <span class="os-card-x">✕</span>
        <h3>A discussion forum</h3>
        <p>No debates. No open comments. Structure and evidence over opinion.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card os-card-dim">
        <span class="os-card-x">✕</span>
        <h3>A reporting tool</h3>
        <p>Not for reporting individual issues. For documenting systemic patterns based on evidence.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 4: WHY IT MATTERS -->
<div class="os-section" id="matters">
  <div class="os-section-inner">
    <h2 class="os-section-label">Why this matters</h2>
    <div class="os-matter-grid">
      <div class="os-matter-block">
        <p class="os-matter-text">An isolated experience is a data point.</p>
        <p class="os-matter-arrow">↓</p>
        <p class="os-matter-text">Structured observations are evidence.</p>
        <p class="os-matter-arrow">↓</p>
        <p class="os-matter-text">Connected evidence reveals patterns.</p>
        <p class="os-matter-arrow">↓</p>
        <p class="os-matter-text os-matter-strong">Visible patterns let us understand public systems as they really work.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
    <p class="os-section-intro">We do not promise to fix anything. We promise to document what happens — so that whoever decides, investigates, or uses public services has a factual foundation to work from.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- SECTION 5: HOW IT WORKS -->
<div class="os-section os-section-how" id="how">
  <div class="os-section-inner">
    <h2 class="os-section-label">How it works</h2>
    <div class="os-steps">
      <div class="os-step">
        <div class="os-step-num">1</div>
        <div class="os-step-content">
          <h3>Observe</h3>
          <p>You identify a real problem in a public service — something that happened, not an assumption.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="os-step-connector"></div>
      <div class="os-step">
        <div class="os-step-num">2</div>
        <div class="os-step-content">

          <p>You describe what happened, who was affected, which system is involved, and what the impact is.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="os-step-connector"></div>
      <div class="os-step">
        <div class="os-step-num">3</div>
        <div class="os-step-content">
          <h3>Evidence</h3>
          <p>You attach verifiable sources — news, reports, public data, legislation, or documented cases.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="os-step-connector"></div>
      <div class="os-step">
        <div class="os-step-num">4</div>
        <div class="os-step-content">
          <h3>Connect</h3>
          <p>Related observations are linked — revealing patterns that would not be visible in isolation.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

<!-- SECTION 6: REAL EXAMPLES -->
<div class="os-section" id="examples">
  <div class="os-section-inner">
    <h2 class="os-section-label">Real examples</h2>
    <p class="os-section-intro">This is what OpenState documents. Structured observations, with evidence, about problems that affect millions of people.</p>
    <div class="os-examples">
      <div class="os-example-card">
        <div class="os-example-tag os-example-tag-health">Healthcare</div>
        <h3>8 months for an appointment</h3>
        <p>Patients wait an average of 8 months for a first specialist consultation in the public health system. 15% give up before the appointment. Conditions worsen or they resort to private care.</p>
        <div class="os-example-evidence">
          <span class="os-example-evidence-label">Evidence:</span>
          <span>ERS Report 2024 · Público article · SIGIC data</span>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="os-example-card">
        <div class="os-example-tag os-example-tag-education">Education</div>
        <h3>12 days without class per year</h3>
        <p>When a teacher is absent, the class has no lesson. An average of 12 days/year per class without lessons in public primary schools. 60% of schools report teacher shortages.</p>
        <div class="os-example-evidence">
          <span class="os-example-evidence-label">Evidence:</span>
          <span>CNE Report 2023 · DN article · DGEEC data</span>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="os-example-card">
        <div class="os-example-tag os-example-tag-digital">Digital Services</div>
        <h3>3 portals, 3 addresses</h3>
        <p>Citizen updates their address on Social Security portal. On the Tax Authority portal, the old address still shows. On the Health Service portal, a different address again. Same information, three times, three systems.</p>
        <div class="os-example-evidence">
          <span class="os-example-evidence-label">Evidence:</span>
          <span>AMA Report 2023 · Expresso article · eGov survey</span>
        </div>
      </div>

<!-- SECTION 7: PRINCIPLES -->
<div class="os-section os-section-principles" id="principles">
  <div class="os-section-inner">
    <h2 class="os-section-label">Principles</h2>
    <div class="os-cards">
      <div class="os-card os-card-principle">
        <h3>Neutrality</h3>
        <p>No ideology. No parties. No agendas. The focus is what happens, not what should happen.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card os-card-principle">
        <h3>Evidence</h3>
        <p>Every observation requires verifiable sources. Without evidence, there is no observation.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card os-card-principle">
        <h3>Transparency</h3>
        <p>All documentation is open. Anyone can verify, question, and contribute.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card os-card-principle">
        <h3>Structure</h3>
        <p>Consistent format, factual language, clear organisation. Structure over opinion.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card os-card-principle">
        <h3>Open knowledge</h3>
        <p>What is documented belongs to everyone. No paywalls, no restrictions, no gatekeeping.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card os-card-principle">
        <h3>Long-term</h3>
        <p>OpenState is a decades-long project, not a months-long one. Value grows with time and accumulation.</p>
      </div>
    </div>

<!-- SECTION 8: CONTRIBUTE -->
<div class="os-section os-section-cta" id="contribute">
  <div class="os-section-inner">
    <h2 class="os-section-label">Contribute</h2>
    <p class="os-cta-text">Anyone can contribute to OpenState. No technical background needed. If you have observed a public service problem, you can document it.</p>
    <div class="os-cards">
      <div class="os-card">
        <h3>Structured format</h3>
        <p>Every observation follows a format: what happens, who is affected, evidence, system involved, and impact.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card">
        <h3>Evidence required</h3>
        <p>News articles, official reports, public data, legislation, or documented real cases. No evidence, no observation.</p>
      </div>
      <div class="os-card">
        <h3>Clarity &amp; neutrality</h3>
        <p>Factual language only. No political framing. No personal data. Observations and solutions are strictly separated.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
