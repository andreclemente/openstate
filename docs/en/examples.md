## Example 1 — Health

**Title:** Excessive waiting time for specialist appointments

**What:** Patients wait an average of 8 months for a first specialist consultation.

**Where:** Public health centers and hospitals.

**When:** Recurring, worsened in the last 2 years.

**Evidence:**
- Average wait: 240 days
- 15% of patients give up before the appointment
- European average: 45 days

**Impact:** Loss of quality of life, worsening of health conditions.

**Proposal:** Integrated primary-secondary care system with clinical priority criteria.

## Example 2 — Education

**Title:** Lack of substitute teachers interrupts classes

**What:** When a teacher is absent, the class has no lesson because there is no automatic substitution.

**Where:** Public primary schools.

**When:** On every teacher absence, potentially 5-10% of the school year.

**Evidence:**
- Average 12 days/year per class without lessons due to no substitute
- 60% of schools report teacher shortages at any given time

**Impact:** Learning loss, inequality between schools with and without resources.

**Proposal:** Regional pool of substitute teachers, callable within 24h.

## Example 3 — Transport

**Title:** Bus schedules don't match reality

**What:** Published schedules don't reflect actual arrival times.

**Where:** Urban bus network in a mid-sized city.

**When:** Daily.

**Evidence:**
- 40% of buses are more than 10 minutes late
- Real-time displays often offline or incorrect

**Impact:** Distrust in public transport, increased car usage.

**Proposal:** GPS-based real-time displays + reliable arrival prediction app.
